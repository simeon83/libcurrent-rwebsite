LIBCURRENT RENEWABLE exists to provide reliable, safe, affordable, and sustainable electrical and solar energy solutions that improve the lives of individuals, families, businesses, and communities across Liberia.

Our Story

Powering Progress, One Installation At A Time

LIBCURRENT RENEWABLE was founded to close a simple but urgent gap: Liberian homes, businesses, and industries deserve electrical work and solar systems built to last — not quick fixes that fail in the rainy season.

Our engineers and technicians bring hands-on field experience across residential wiring, commercial power systems, and solar installations, backed by quality-tested materials and a safety-first process on every job site.

Today, we serve homeowners, businesses, construction companies, government offices, and NGOs across Monrovia and beyond — with the same standard on every project, big or small.
